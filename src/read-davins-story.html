Davin's Story

Davin was just 9 months old when he contracted bacterial meningitis. While the illness took away many of his physical abilities, it never diminished his joy, resilience, or bright spirit.

Today, Davin is a medically fragile teenager receiving hospice care. He requires around-the-clock support, and providing that level of care truly takes a village. Despite the challenges, Davin remains an absolute delight to his family. They treasure simple moments together, like walks along the beach path in his wheelchair and outings to the movies.

Caring for a child with complex medical needs is a labor of love, but it is also physically and emotionally demanding. Families who provide 24/7 nursing-level care need opportunities to rest, recharge, and tend to their own well-being.

Having a safe, compassionate place where Davin can stay gives his family priceless peace of mind. It offers them the chance to do things many families take for granted, such as visiting Davin's brother in college on the East Coast, attending to their own health needs, or simply taking time to rest. Most importantly, they can do so knowing that Davin is receiving expert, loving care in a true home-away-from-home.

For families like Davin's, respite care is not a luxury. It is a beacon of hope.
