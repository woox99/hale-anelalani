Providing Community Engagement

Stronger Together for Hawaii’s Keiki

Hale ‘Anelalani is more than a home-it is a community coming together for Hawaii’s keiki and ohana. By bringing together families, healthcare providers, community organizations, schools, businesses, and local leaders, we hope to build a stronger network of support for children living with serious and complex medical conditions throughout Hawaii.

Connecting Community. Strengthening Ohana.

Our hope is to create a community where families caring for medically fragile and seriously ill children feel seen, connected, and surrounded by support.

Where Community Becomes ‘Ohana

It will be a place of connection and support, partnering with local organizations, welcoming volunteers, and hosting family and remembrance events. We strive to extend compassion beyond our walls and build a strong circle of community care for the entire 'ohana

A message from the founder

No family caring for a seriously ill child should ever feel alone on their journey. My hope is that Hale ‘Anelalani brings our community together around these ohana -creating connections, opening doors, and reminding them that they are surrounded by people who care.
