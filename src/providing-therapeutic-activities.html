Providing Therapeutic Activities

Joy Beyond the Diagnosis

At Hale ‘Anelalani, care will be more than meeting medical needs. Therapeutic activities will be thoughtfully designed around each child’s abilities, interests, and comfort-creating opportunities for movement, creativity, sensory exploration, connection, and most importantly, joy.

Helping Every Keiki Find Joy in the Moments that Matter

Through art, music, and play therapy, we will offer therapeutic activities that promote healing, expression, and joy for keiki and their 'ohana

A message from the founder

A serious illness should never take away a child’s opportunity to experience the simple joys of childhood. My hope is that every keiki who comes through our door finds something that makes them smile, helps them feel included, and reminds them that they are a kid first!
