Lennox's Story

Our sweet boy Lennox had a rough start to life. He suffered lack of oxygen to his brain which has left him with the diagnosis of Hypoxic-ischemic Encephalopathy (HIE). As parents we were willing to do everything and anything to help make sure that he lives the most normal, loving life.

He has been receiving palliative hospice care since he came home from the NICU. I (Mom) made the decision to leave my full time job to be Lennox’s full time caregiver.

As a family we love going for walks and going on family adventures around the island. He’s a spunky kid that was left with no facial expressions due to his diagnosis, but you can still see that twinkle in his eyes and the joy in his heart.

With so much love, learning, and trials, our now two year old has improved greatly but still remains very medically fragile and needs around the clock care. We as parents do need a break from time to time to focus on our own wellbeing, our marriage, and ways to improve our care for our amazing boy.

Hale Anelalani will help our family blossom and be the best versions we can be to care for Lennox. Our village is amazing, but sometimes you just need a little more help.

With the Aloha and kokua from Hale Anelalani, we wholeheartedly trust that our son will be safe and very well taken care of. Exactly how he would be cared for at home. This second home for our medically fragile keiki is beneficial for families to thrive.
