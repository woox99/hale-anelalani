Providing Breavement Support

Walking Beside ‘Ohana Through Grief

The loss of a child changes a family forever. At Hale ‘Anelalani, our care will not end when a child’s life does. We will continue to walk alongside parents, siblings, and loved ones as they navigate grief-offering a familiar place of connection, remembrance, and support for as long as they need it.

Remembered Always. Supported Always.

  • Sibling bereavement supportgrade_outline

  • Grief Groups and family gatheringsgrade_outline
  • Legacy and Remembrance Activitiesgrade_outline
  • Anniversary and Milestone Supportgrade_outline
  • Spiritual and Cultural Supportgrade_outline
  • Ongoing Connection to Resources and Community Supportgrade_outline

There is no timeline for grieving a child. Hale ‘Anelalani will strive to remain a place where families can return, remember their keiki, speak their name, share their story , and know they are still surrounded by a community that remembers with them.

Because Love Doesn’t End When a Child’s Life Does

We will offer compassionate grief support before, during and after a child's passing, including counseling, sibling support, memory making and ongoing outreach. We walk alongside families, honoring each child's life while supporting healing in their own time.

A message from the founder

When a child dies, a family’s need for love and support does not end - it changes. My hope is that Hale ‘Anelalani will always be a place where ‘ohana can return, where their child’s name is remembered, their story continues to be shared, and they never have to walk through grief alone.
