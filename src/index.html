    Hale Anelalani is a home where families can breathe and children are cared for with Aloha.

Hawaii’s First non-profit pediatric respite and hospice home, created with the vision of surrounding keiki and their ‘ohana with hope, healing and love. Guided by light and love we have come together to build a home here in Hawaii that is not only a place for compassionate end-of-life care, but also a sanctuary for respite—a place where families of seriously ill children can come to rest, recharge, and breathe.

This will be a home where children can thrive in a setting filled with warmth, joy, and connection. A place where expert care is always present, allowing parents to step out of their medical roles, even for a short time, and simply hold their child, laugh with them, or take a much-needed break without fear or guilt. A place where it’s okay to rest.

It will also be a home where siblings are fully seen and embraced—where they are included in care, supported in their emotions, and valued for the vital role they play in their sibling’s life. Here, families won’t be divided by illness, they will be brought closer together.

For some, this home will provide a peaceful, loving space at the end of life. For many others, it will be a lifeline in the middle of an ongoing journey—a place of renewal, support, and community when the weight of caregiving becomes overwhelming.

"A home not just for the hardest days—but for all the days in between."

This vision is about giving families something they rarely receive, relief without worry, support without judgment, and moments of peace in the midst of complexity.

With your support, we can create a place where children are cared for with expertise and love, where parents can finally exhale, and where families can reconnect in the ways that matter most.

Support for all the Children of Hawaii

Pilina (Who we serve)

Every child deserves the care and support they need to thrive. Families caring for children with life-limiting conditions need more than medical treatment—they need a place to rest, recharge, and find support.

  • 93% of families have no access to a children’s respite home.grade_outline
  • The U.S. falls far behind other nations—if we matched the U.K., Canada, and Australia by population, we’d have 266 respite homes today. Instead, we have just seven. grade_outline
  • 44,000 children and youth in Hawaii that have special health care needs (MCHB TVIS Data 2021) grade_outline

What we do

Hana No'eau

Respite Care

We will provide expert care for medically fragile keiki in a safe, nurturing environment. While families are able to rest and recharge the keiki enjoy fun, adaptive activities and meaningful moments supported by experienced pediatric staff.

Therapeutic Activities

Through art, music, and play therapy, we will offer therapeutic activities that promote healing, expression, and joy for keiki and their 'ohana

Learn More arrow_forward_ios
Community Engagement

It will be a place of connection and support, partnering with local organizations, welcoming volunteers, and hosting family and remembrance events. We strive to extend compassion beyond our walls and build a strong circle of community care for the entire 'ohana.

Learn More arrow_forward_ios
End-of-Life Care

We will provide peaceful, comfort-focused care that honors each child's life. Surrounded by warmth and support, families are never alone as we ensure dignity, love, and meaningful moments in their child's final days.

Learn More arrow_forward_ios
Bereavement Support

We will offer compassionate grief support before, during and after a child's passing, including counseling, sibling support, memory making and ongoing outreach. We walk alongside families, honoring each child's life while supporting healing in their own time.
