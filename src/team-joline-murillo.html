"Together we've impacted the lives of over 200 million vulnerable children by tackling the root causes of poverty."

Joline Murillo

RN, BSN, CHPPN
Founder / Executive Director

Born and raised in Kāneʻohe, Hawaiʻi has always been more than the place I call home—it is who I am. My ʻohana, my faith, my community, and my culture have shaped the person I have become, and I have always carried a deep desire to give back to our people and care for the place and community that raised and nurtured me. For more than 20 years, I have had the honor of caring for the keiki of Hawaiʻi as a registered nurse, with much of that time spent in the Pediatric Intensive Care Unit at Kapiʻolani Hospital. I have stood beside families in some of their most vulnerable moments—celebrating victories, navigating uncertainty, and holding them through unimaginable grief.

My journey eventually led me into pediatric hospice and palliative care, where I’ve become Hawaiʻi’s only certified hospice and palliative pediatric nurse: a reflection of my deep commitment to serving children with serious illness and their ʻohana. In this work, I began to see something deeper: a wider perspective. Beyond the medicine and the monitors, I saw parents who rarely had the opportunity to rest. Parents who became full-time caregivers, medical experts, and constant advocates for their child—often sacrificing the simple gift of just being Mom or Dad. I saw siblings who loved fiercely, yet too often found themselves on the sidelines.

I have walked alongside these families for decades. I have witnessed their incredible strength, but I have also seen their exhaustion. These experiences have shaped not only the nurse I am, but the mission I carry in my heart.

What if we could give these families a place to simply be?

That question became my calling—and the seed from which Hale ‘Anelalani began.

My faith and my ʻohana have been the foundation beneath this dream and continue to give me strength in its pursuit. I am deeply grateful to Ke Akua for entrusting me with this opportunity to help our lāhui.

Hale ‘Anelalani is more than a place we hope to build. It is a promise that no one will have to walk this journey alone.
