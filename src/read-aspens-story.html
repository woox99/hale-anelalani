Aspen's Testimonial

Joline has been there for us ever since Aspen was three years old, when she was critically ill.

Whenever Aspen became sick and I felt scared or uncertain, Joline was always there to listen, give us thoughtful and knowledgeable advice, and help us through those difficult moments. She has always cared for Aspen with so much compassion and has been there for me personally whenever I was hurting or struggling.

Joline has been an incredible source of support for our family. She is always genuinely caring, compassionate, and willing to go above and beyond to help us.

She is so much more than a caregiver to us. She is someone we trust deeply and someone who has become truly irreplaceable in our lives.

The reason we are able to live with more peace of mind today is because of Joline’s knowledge, compassion, and love.

We are incredibly grateful to have Joline in our lives, and we know that the love and care she gives to the children and their families at Hale Anelalani will make a meaningful difference in so many lives.
