Jasmin Jensen

Medical Director

My name is Dr. Jasmin Jensen; I came to Hawaii in 2014 after completing a pediatric hematology-oncology fellowship in Utah. I completed a Hospice and Palliative Care Fellowship in Columbus Ohio, and I have also completed a fellowship in pediatric hematology and oncology. I have cared for medically complex children, many with rare and life limiting chronic conditions throughout my training and career. I grew up in a small town in rural Utah with a large extended family and this upbringing instilled in me an understanding that community and family are sources of strength and resilience.

My personal experiences with health and illness as well as my relationship with many patients and their families have shaped how I understand my role as a healer, I have learned that when confronted with illness we still must live our lives and simultaneously be a patient.

A diagnosis of a serious illness, especially one that impacts a child, will bring with it many decisions that must be made. I believe that as a pediatric palliative care doctor my job is to make sure that my patients and their family have all the information, they need to make some of the most important decisions that they will ever make and to support them as they travel down paths that can be long and winding. My job is to help patients and families live the best lives they can; to help them figure out how to their experience with a complicated disease in a beloved family member will become part of their family’s story. I believe that home is more than a physical place and families deserve the option to care for their children at home whenever this is a safe and feasible option. This seems like a simple fact but it can be a complicated goal that all to often feels out of reach for many families in our state, this reality has motivated me to advocate for improved access and resources for programs like pediatric concurrent care and outpatient pediatric palliative care.

When I am not at work, I enjoy spending time with friends and family. Hawaii is now my home and I am passionate about exploring this amazing state and learning about its natural and human history. I enjoy music, literature, film, theatre and arts and crafts-most years I make Christmas gifts for my friends and co-workers. I am a documentary nerd which makes me a great person to have on a Trivial Pursuits team! My favorite place in the world is my home on Oahu where I live with an ever-growing personal zoo and various family members who take turns visiting me from the mainland.
