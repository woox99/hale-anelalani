Providing Respite Care

Rest that Strengthens the ‘Ohana

Caring for a child with complex medical needs can require around-the-clock attention. Hale ‘Anelalani will provide families with a trusted place where their keiki can receive compassionate, skilled care while parents and caregivers have time to rest, reconnect, attend to other family needs, or simply take a much-needed break.

Our respite services will be designed around each child’s unique medical, developmental, emotional, and social needs. During their stay, children can continue their individualized care routines while also enjoying opportunities for play, creativity, sensory experiences, outdoor activities and meaningful connection.

Because Caring for the Caregiver is Part of Caring for the Child.

Rest for ‘Ohana. Joy for Keiki

We will provide expert care for medically fragile keiki in a safe, nurturing environment. While families are able to rest and recharge the keiki enjoy fun, adaptive activities and meaningful moments supported by experienced pediatric staff.

A message from the founder

Caring for a child with a serious illness is a journey filled with incredible love, but even the strongest families need time to rest. My hope is that Hale ‘Anelalani becomes the place where parents can finally exhale, knowing their child is safe, loved, and experiencing the joy of simply being a child.
