Ho’ohana

Our Mission is to provide compassionate ohana centered care for keiki with life limiting conditions focusing on enhancing their quality of life and helping their ohana in every stage of their journey. We are dedicated to providing physical, emotional, and spiritual care while fostering dignity, love and joy. Through personalized care, education and advocacy, we aim to create a nurturing environment where keiki can thrive and their ohana can feel empowered and supported.

Our vision is to create a compassionate sanctuary where keiki with life limiting conditions and their ohana find comfort, peace, hope and joy. We envision a hale where they can gather to receive holistic, ohana-centered care that provides them with the best quality of life.

RN, BSN, CHPPN

Joline Murillo

Founder & Executive Director

For more than 20 years, I have had the honor of caring for the keiki of Hawaii as a registered nurse, most of that time spent in the Pediatric Intensive Care Unit at Kapi'olani Hospital. I have stood beside families in their most vulnerable moments, celebrating small victories, navigating uncertainty, and holding them through unimaginable grief. These experiences have shaped not only the nurse I am, but the mission I carry in my heart.

In the PICU and most recently working in pediatric hospice and palliative care, I began to see something deeper. Beyond the medicine, beyond the monitors, I saw parents who never got to rest. Parents who became full-time caregivers, medical experts, and constant advocates for their child—often at the expense of simply being mom and dad. I saw siblings who loved fiercely but were too often on the sidelines. And I kept asking myself: What if we could give these families a place to just be?

That question became my calling.

I have walked alongside these families for decades. I have seen their strength—but I have also seen their exhaustion. And I know we can do better for them.

Hale ‘Anelalani was created with the intent to help these keiki and ‘ohana with love and support throughout their entire journey. My faith and my ‘ohana have been the backbone to helping create this dream for our lahui. I am so grateful to Ke Akua for the opportunity to walk alongside these ‘ohana.
