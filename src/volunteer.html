Laulima

Many hands - One heart - One hui

Hale Anelalani is being built through laulima — families, healthcare professionals, volunteers, community partners, and supporters each bringing their time, talents, and aloha to create something Hawaiʻi’s keiki and families deeply need.

Whether you share a professional skill, help at a community event, lend a hand with fundraising, care for our future gardens and spaces, or simply give your time — there is a place for your hands in this mission.

"E Laulima Kākou"

Together, we can build a home of comfort, connection, and aloha for Hawaiʻi’s medically fragile children and their ʻohana.

Join our Ohana!

Get Started Volunteering

We appreciate your interest in supporting our mission. To learn more about volunteer opportunities, email us at info@haleanalalani.org. We’ll be happy to provide more information about current opportunities and how you can get involved.
