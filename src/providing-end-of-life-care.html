Providing End-of-Life Care

A Place of Comfort, Love and Togetherness

When a child is nearing the end of life, families deserve choices about where and how those precious moments are spent. Hale ‘Anelalani will provide a peaceful, home-like setting where keiki can be surrounded by the people they love while receiving compassionate, specialized end-of-life care.

Our care will focus on comfort, dignity and honoring what matters most to each child and ohana. Families will have the space and support to simply be together-away from the clinical environment of a hospital and surrounded by warmth, familiarity, and aloha.

More Time for Love. More Space for Family.

At Hale ‘Anelalani, end-of-life care will be about more than a child’s final days. It will be about making every moment matter-bringing comfort when it is needed most and creating a place where families can focus on what is most important: being together.

When Every Moment Matters Most

We will provide peaceful, comfort-focused care that honors each child's life. Surrounded by warmth and support, families are never alone as we ensure dignity, love, and meaningful moments in their child's final days.

A message from the founder

In a child’s final moments, families deserve a place where they can simply hold their child, love them, and be together. My hope is that no ‘ohana must spend those sacred final moments in a hospital, but instead a home away from home.
